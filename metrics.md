
## Execution Quality

**1. On-Time Delivery Rate** Formula: `(Tasks completed within estimated time / Total tasks completed) × 100` Target: 75–85%
**2. Carry-over Rate** (inverse metric — lower is better) Formula: `(Tasks rolled to next sprint / Tasks committed) × 100` Target: <15%
**3. Defect Density** Formula: `Bugs reported in last 30 days post-release / tasks delivered` Target: < x threshold
**4. Code Review Rework Rate** Formula: `(PRs requiring >2 revision rounds / Total PRs) × 100` Target: <20%
