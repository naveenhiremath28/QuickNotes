
## **Why Finternet?**


Ability to take different kind of assets convert them into tokens and have high speed engines to transact and untokenize at the end

individuals of businesses have the ability to unify for different assets

3 Uses:
1. user centric: Because user want more control over their assets
2. Unified: Connects all financial services on one interconnected network.
3. Universal: Open and accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Assets can be any property, bonds, paintings money -> all of the asset classes should come together (Assets like 1. User controlled assets i.e NFT 2. Attested Assets like paintings, 3. Registered Assets like properties, cars etc. 4. Regulated Assets i.e crypto currencies)

why tokens?

one of the intention is to bring cryptography in our regulated world where we have rules, regulations, KYC, anti-money londering
finternet tries to combines best regulated world and best tokenization technology

Finternet uses basic constructs of tokens and enables universal interoper abilities and composability

Interoper abilities: tokens can be bought and sold by different ledgers
Composabilty: combine different kind of assets to construct full transaction


Highlevel of Finternet Architecture

1. Keep user/business at the center
2. give Application to the user
3.  token managers: we have existing actors (bank, companies, asset managers)
	1. because they take their respective asset and tokenize it, eg bank can tokenize the deposit, asset manager tokenize mutual funds
4. Unified Ledger: tokenized assets, unified ledger doesnt specify single ledger (etherium, solana etc) but it can be multiple ledgers of different technologies
	1. there will also have protocols for inter ledger transactions
5. Trust Providers: In todays world if you buy an art work somebody will gives it as a genuine piece of art
	1. Tokens immutability itself is one kind of trust
	2. consider a bank tokenize the deposite so token contains all the attributes of asset since token is immutable, it adds trust
Note:
All asset token transaction done in same standard way, irrespective of asset class it can be converted to standard way

The Finternet interconnects multiple ecosystems, much like the internet, putting 8B+ people and 300M businesses at the center of their financial lives.
